## ⭐ Featured: goDutch!

**Split the bill with your friends in seconds — just scan the receipt.**

My most recent project and my favorite technical challenge so far. goDutch! reads the receipt with **Apple Intelligence**, falls back to **OCR** on iPhones without it, and lets friends split items by dragging playful **SpriteKit** bubbles. It's live on the App Store with a **5.0 rating** and cuts the time to split a bill by about **75%**.

[View project](./goDutch) · [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/godutch/id6807981022)

## Project Overview

### Terminal Typing

A typing game that runs in the terminal, with sound effects built with AVFoundation. My first Swift project at the Academy.

**Stack:** Swift, AVFoundation · [View project](./Terminal-Typing-Game)

### JAT Data

An inventory and sales tracker for self-employed workers and small business owners. My first app published on the App Store, built in a team of 3.

**Stack:** SwiftUI, Core Data, MVVM · [View project](./JAT-Data) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/jat-data/id6747201031)

### Habits — Improve

A habit-building app with daily goals, reminders, streaks and a GitHub-style pixel calendar. My first solo app published on the App Store.

**Stack:** SwiftUI, Core Data, MVVM, local notifications · [View project](./Habitos-Improve) · [App Store](https://lnkd.in/eDsYAZxu)

### Moony

Helps children with sensory hypersensitivity and ASD get familiar with sounds from unfamiliar places, through interactive SpriteKit scenes. Built in a team of 5.

**Stack:** SpriteKit, CoreHaptics, Core Data · [View project](./Moony) · [App Store](https://lnkd.in/gyQySHMg)

### Kid Task

Brings children with ASD into the household routine: tasks created together, a mascot that reacts to completed tasks and personalized rewards. I implemented all 10 screens in one week as the team's only developer.

**Stack:** SwiftUI, Core Data, AVFoundation · [View project](./Kid-Task) · [TestFlight (beta)](https://testflight.apple.com/join/S1fcRPHC)

### Articula

A wrist physiotherapy app that detects movement in real time using the iPhone's gyroscope and accelerometer, with an exercise validated by a physiotherapist.

**Stack:** UIKit, Core Motion, Core Data · [View project](./Articula)

### goDutch!

A bill-splitting app that scans the receipt with Apple Intelligence, with an OCR fallback and drag-and-drop splitting with SpriteKit bubbles. Built in a team of 4.

**Stack:** SwiftUI, Apple Intelligence, OCR, SpriteKit, SwiftData · [View project](./goDutch) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/godutch/id6807981022)
